Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 3, 2026

Freezme ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: Email address, name, date of birth, and profile photos you provide during sign-up.
  • Profile data: Bio, interests, preferences, and vibe settings you add to your profile.
  • Location data: Approximate location when you use the Paths feature (only while the app is in use).
  • Usage data: Interactions within the app such as swipes, matches, and messages.
  • Device data: Device type, OS version, and push notification token for delivering notifications.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To show you relevant match suggestions
  • To enable the Paths nearby feature
  • To send push notifications about matches and messages
  • To improve and personalise the app experience
  • To enforce our Terms of Service and prevent abuse

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Service providers: AWS (photo storage), Google (push notifications via FCM), Apple (push notifications via APNs).
  • Legal requirements: When required by law or to protect user safety.

4. Location Data

Location is only collected when you actively use the Paths feature and have granted permission. We store your last known geohash (not exact coordinates) on our servers, which expires after 60 minutes of inactivity. You can disable location access at any time in your device settings.

5. Photos

Profile photos are stored securely on AWS S3. They are accessible only via private URLs and are deleted when you delete your account.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, matches, messages, and photos are permanently deleted within 30 days.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@freezme.in.

8. Children

Freezme is not intended for anyone under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Security

We use industry-standard encryption (TLS in transit, encrypted storage at rest) to protect your data. No system is 100% secure; we encourage you to use a strong, unique password.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via in-app notification or email.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, email privacy@freezme.in.
