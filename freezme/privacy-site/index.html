Freezme Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2025

Freezme (“we”, “our”, or “us”) operates the Freezme mobile application (“App”).
This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Category Details Purpose
Account information Email address, display name, age range, onboarding preferences Create and secure your account, personalise the experience
Profile content Bio, prompts, photos you upload Show your vibe to daily pool members
Usage data Likes, skips, matches, feature usage, crash logs Improve matchmaking quality, detect abuse, maintain reliability
Device data Device model, OS version, app version, push token Diagnostics, performance analytics, push notifications

2. How We Collect Data

We only collect data you provide directly inside the App or that is generated when you use the App. We use Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, and Firebase Storage (all operated by Google LLC) to process and store this data securely.

3. How We Use Data

  1. Provide the service – authenticate you, build your profile, surface matches, and deliver notifications.
  2. Safety and moderation – investigate suspicious activity, enforce community guidelines, and prevent spam.
  3. Product improvement – analyse aggregated usage patterns to decide which features to build next.
  4. Support – respond to questions sent to support@freezme.app.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with third parties for advertising.

4. Account & Data Deletion

You can request full account deletion any time:

  1. Open the Freezme app → Settings → Help & Support → “Delete my account”.
  2. Or email support@freezme.app from the address you used to sign up.

We’ll confirm the request within 48 hours and permanently erase:

  • Profile details (intent, bio, preferences)
  • Uploaded photos and matches
  • Authentication tokens in Firebase Auth

Non-identifiable analytics/counter data may remain for up to 13 months for fraud prevention.

5. Data Retention

  • Account and profile data are kept while your account remains active.
  • You may request deletion at any time via the in‑app settings or by emailing support@freezme.app.
  • Crash and diagnostic logs are retained for up to 13 months.

6. Your Rights & Controls

  • Access / Update – edit your profile from Settings.
  • Delete – request deletion via Settings → Privacy or email support@freezme.app.
  • Notifications – control push notifications in Settings or the device OS.
  • Data export – email us to receive a copy of the data associated with your account.

7. International Transfers

Your data may be processed on servers outside of your country (including the United States) because we use Firebase. We rely on Google’s security and compliance commitments to safeguard transfers.

8. Children

Freezme is for adults 18+ only. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we learn we collected information from someone under 18, we delete it immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced inside the App and the “Last updated” date will be revised.

10. Contact Us

Questions about privacy? Email support@freezme.app.
