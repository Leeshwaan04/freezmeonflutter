## Description

**Freezme is the dating and social app designed for *right now*.**

Tired of endless swiping, conversation dead-ends, and "maybe next week"? Freezme cuts through the noise to get you offline and into the real world. We focus on Recency and Proximity—connecting you with people who are active, nearby, and looking to make plans *tonight*.

### ❄️ How It Works

**1. The Daily Batch**
Every day at noon, your "Daily Pool" works fresh. We curate a limited selection of profiles based on your vibe, interests, and location. Quality over quantity.

**2. Tonight Mode**
See who is actually free tonight. Post your "Path" (e.g., "Grab drinks at 8 PM?" or "Walk in the park?") and find others who want to join. Spontaneity is back.

**3. Break the Ice**
Our unique Icebreaker games and prompts help you skip the awkward small talk and get straight to the connection.

**4. Blind Dates**
Feeling adventurous? Enter the "Blind Queue." We'll pair you with a compatible match for a guaranteed conversation. No photos, just vibes—until you decide to reveal.

### 🌟 Premium Features
- **Super Vibes:** Stand out to the people you really like.
- **See Who Likes You:** Get a head start on your matches.
- **Travel Mode:** Make plans in a new city before you even arrive.

**Why Freezme?**
Because life is happening tonight. Whether you're looking for a serious relationship, a casual date, or just a new friend to hang out with, Freezme is the fastest way to make it happen.

**Download Freezme today. The ice isn't going to break itself.**
